Donate and Support

Send a young man who could not otherwise go.

The $320 pays for the weekend itself: the bus, the food, the site, the gear. Nobody is paid. When a family cannot cover it, the difference comes from people like you, and a young man who would have stayed home gets on the bus.

Give

By e-transfer, to one address.

Send an Interac e-transfer from your bank to the address below. Put "YMAW support" and your name in the message so we can thank you. If you are paying for a particular young man's registration, put his name in the message instead, the same way a family does, and we will apply it to his registration.

E-transfer to info@ymaw.com Message: "YMAW support" and your name, or the young man's name

A gift of $320 covers one registration in full; any amount helps, and smaller gifts pool toward the same thing. When a family ticks the assistance box on the registration form, a man from the team calls them privately and works it out. Your gift is what he has to work with.

Questions about giving, or about a larger gift: info@ymaw.com.

Give time

Can't make the weekend? There is still a job with your name on it.

Truck load-up

The Thursday before the weekend, September 10. Everything the weekend needs goes into one truck, and it takes many hands.

Strike team offload

Sunday evening after the weekend, in Abbotsford. Meet the truck, unload it, and put the weekend back in the locker.

Shop for supplies

Help prep the gear and essentials for both the main weekend and the Friday staff training.

Spread the word

Tell a family with a young man aged 12 to 17. Share the site. A word from a man they trust carries further than anything we post.

To take one of these on, tick it under "availability" when you register as production, or email info@ymaw.com.

Sponsor a young man you know

Bring him yourself.

The most direct way to support a young man is to be the man who brings him. A sponsor, whether father, uncle, guardian or family friend, registers alongside the young men he is responsible for, pays the same $320 each, and comes as part of the production team.

You will complete the screening and the Friday training like every other man, and you will spend the weekend in service to all of the young men, not only yours. That is the part many men do not expect, and the part they come back for.
