## Facts you may use

Each line is on the site or in a named document. Say them in these words or shorter.

- **What:** Young Men's Adventure Weekend (YMAW), run by the Young Men's Adventure Weekend Society of BC. A rite of passage, not a summer camp. Volunteer men, every year **since 1990**. Nobody is paid.
- **When:** Friday **September 11 to Sunday September 13, 2026**. Friday evening to Sunday afternoon.
- **Where:** a wilderness site in the **Squamish region, BC**. Say "Squamish region", not a place name.
- **Who:** **young men aged 12 to 17**, of every background. No camping experience needed.
- **Cost:** **$320 per person, and everyone pays the same** — young men, sponsors and production men. It covers transport from the pickup points, snacks Friday, three meals and snacks Saturday, breakfast and lunch Sunday, camping accommodation in shelters the teams build, and the production team. Bus stop to bus stop.
- **Transport:** bus pickups in **Burnaby, Langley and Squamish**, and a **carpool from Vancouver Island**. Exact stops and times go to registered families.
- **Assistance:** choose "Request financial assistance" on the payment step and register anyway; a member of the team follows up personally and privately. The site's wording: "We have never turned a young man away for money" (weekend.html, faq.html).
- **Paying:** card, or Interac e-transfer to info@ymaw.com with the reference code. Nothing is charged until you choose to pay. Registration takes about five minutes (faq.html).
- **Phone-free:** phones go in the bag for the weekend, the men's included (faq.html).
- **Non-denominational.** Intentional about character: responsibility, integrity, community and self-discovery (faq.html).
- **Who runs it:** screened volunteer men. Every first-time staffer shadows before he leads. Friday-morning training on site before the young men arrive. Never fewer than two men with any young man; no man is ever alone with a young man; anyone, of any age, can call "safety" and everything stops; no drugs, alcohol or tobacco; medical information travels with the safety lead and nowhere else; what is said in the circle stays in the circle (weekend.html, team.html).
- **The shape of it (weekend.html):** he rides a bus north with young men he has never met; hikes in at dusk carrying his own gear, usually in the dark; is placed on a team that builds its own shelter and chooses its own leader; Saturday is the Quest Stations, challenges of strength, nerve, problem-solving and trust that no one can finish alone; Saturday night the men speak first and no one is made to speak; Sunday a ceremony, Future Plans written in his own hand, a ball game against the men with almost no rules, and the walk out between two lines of the men who witnessed his weekend.
- **The standards he is coached to (why.html):** Truthful, Excellent, Accountable, Mindful, Service.
- **Sponsors:** a father, uncle, guardian or family friend registers alongside the young men he brings, pays the same $320, and comes as a production man after screening and Friday training (faq.html, donate.html). Form: ymaw.com/register.html#sponsor. Production team: ymaw.com/register.html#production.
- **Other ways to help (donate.html):** truck load-up Thursday September 10; strike team offload Sunday evening in Abbotsford; a $320 e-transfer to info@ymaw.com with "donation" or a young man's name in the message covers one place.
- **How young men arrive (donate.html):** "Most young men arrive because a man they knew told their parents about it." This is the site's own statement and the reason the plan below runs on personal texts and calls.
- **Contact:** **info@ymaw.com** is the only contact that appears anywhere. Instagram @ymaw_official. Facebook facebook.com/ymawofficial.

## 10. Target math — written as ASSUMPTIONS to be checked

Let **N** be tonight's count. The gap is **50 − N**. Every rate below is a guess to be replaced by the real ratio on September 4 and again on September 6. None of it is a fact.

**A1 — Personal text from a man the dad knows:** assume about **1 registration per 8 texts** (range 1 in 5 to 1 in 12). Basis: nothing measured. The site's statement that most young men arrive because a man they knew told their parents (donate.html) supports the *channel*, not the rate.

**A2 — Phone call to a family that attended last year:** assume about **1 in 3** either re-registers or produces one referred registration. Basis: nothing measured.
