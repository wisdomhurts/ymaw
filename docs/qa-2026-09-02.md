## Findings, ranked

### Blocker

**B1. index.html — the "Saturday night, the circle" leg is pushed half off the left edge on every phone width.**
At 390 the `.w-circle` copy block's bounding box is `left −152 … right 192` (width 343 in a 390 viewport); at 360 it is `−138…178`, at 414 `−161…203`, at 430 `−168…211`. The heading reads "day night, / circle.", the paragraph is cut mid-word, and the 2003 archive photo is entirely off-screen (only 2006 and 2025 show). Identical under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`. Desktop is fine.
Mechanism (measured, not guessed): `scrollcraft.css:262 .sc-copy--center { left:50%; translate:-50% -50% }`; on mobile the block's computed `left` becomes `20px` (the gutter) while the `-50%` X translate stays, so the box is shifted left by half its own width. The other two centred blocks (`.w-peak`, `.w-finale`, `site.css:131–132`) override `inset-inline` and sit correctly at `left 14–17`; `.w-circle` (`site.css:84`) has no equivalent override.
Document `scrollWidth` still equals `innerWidth`, so a horizontal-overflow check does **not** catch this — the content is clipped, not scrollable.
Evidence: `index-390-scroll55.png`, `index-390-reduced-motion-scroll55.png`, `verify/index-360-circle.png`, `verify/index-390-circle.png`, `verify/index-414-circle.png`, `verify/index-430-circle.png`; numbers in `verify/measure.json` (`circle@*`).

**T4. register.html has no Open Graph tags; success.html has no description and no OG tags.**
`register.html:1–14` head has `<title>` + description only — no `og:title`/`og:description`/`og:image` (every partial-rendered page has them via `src/partials/head.html:8–10`). This is the page most likely to be shared by parents. `success.html:1–18` has `<title>` and `robots noindex` but no `meta description` and no OG (lower stakes because it is noindex, but a shared "I registered!" link previews blank).

**T5. success.html tells a direct visitor that money was received.**
With no query string (`/success.html`), the script defaults `path` to `card` and shows "He's on the list." + "Payment received. A receipt is on its way from Stripe" (`success.html:79,85`). Anyone landing there from history, a typo or a forwarded bare link is told a payment happened. Also `total` and `who` are echoed straight from the URL (`success.html:83,93–94`; `textContent`, so no XSS, but any amount can be made to display).
Evidence: `success-1440.png`, `success-390.png`.

**T6. Heading-level skips on every page.**
Footer columns use `<h4>` directly after `<h2>` sections (`src/partials/foot.html:9,17`, and the same hand-copied footer in `register.html`), producing `h2 → h4` on weekend/why/team/faq/donate/since1990/register. register.html additionally has `h1 → h3`: the three waiver boxes are `<h3>` (`register.html:225,239,263`) and the step titles are `<legend>`s, which are not headings. Screen-reader outline of register.html is therefore "Register for the weekend. › Participation agreement › YMAW Standards › Media release › Sponsor a young man's place › Asked every year".

**T7. since1990.html shows a dollar amount other than $320.**
`since1990.html:184` / `src/pages/since1990.html:140`: `$125 "per young man and production man"` in the THEN column of the 2011-vs-2026 table. It is clearly historical and labelled, but it is the only non-$320 figure on the site and the brief says $320 only — a human should decide whether the history exception stands.

### Later

Local `assets/p1.webp p3.webp p4.webp p5.webp` (+ `-m`) exist but are unused. `assets/p3–p5.webp` are still the OG images.

**L8. `?canceled=1` behaviour changed today.** `register.js:232–240` now shows the cancellation notice at step 1 (previously jumped to step 4). Deep link `#young-man` from Stripe's `cancel_url` still resolves. Confirm this is intended.
