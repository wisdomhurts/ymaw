Nobody has looked at the pixels for this pass: every line below says only what the description supports, and "verify" means exactly that — someone opens the frame before it ships.*

**Video clips referenced** (a1-NNN) are the 2024 clips surveyed in `.cache/gp/shortlist.md` / `scenes.json`.

## (a) The albums

**a1 · YMAW 2024 (by Dorian) — 135 stills (the album's 102 videos are surveyed separately). GREEN 72 · AMBER 40 · FLAG 20 · CEREMONY 3.** A coastal site: ocean beach with bleached driftwood, pebble shore at twilight with a crescent moon, dense cedar forest, white tarps pitched between huge trunks, a long communal table under green canvas, a rustic mess hall, an archery range, a night fire. The look is high-contrast summer sun through canopy, saturated greens and blues, and cool dusk on the water. It is also the most *personal* album: a long selfie series of a production man with the team (mostly GREEN, adults), the whole-weekend group photos (AMBER; one version includes a woman and is FLAG), and a drum circle / slope sequence that reads as a visitors' moment (FLAG). Its GREEN strength is landscape, the camp build, archery from behind, the long table from the man's back, and the fire in silhouette. Note the CAMP BYNG sign in a1-170 — never publish the site's name.

**a2 · Some YMAW 2019 Photos — 40 stills. GREEN 32 · AMBER 8.** Overcast lake weekend: grey water, misty mountains, a yellow school bus alone on a forest road, kayaks, beach fires at dusk, a huge night bonfire, a mossy old-growth clearing. Descriptions are terse (the describer wrote one line per still), so the AMBER calls here are conservative. Muted, cool palette — the most "field-guide" looking album, and proportionally the most usable.

**a3 · YMAW 2016 Photos — 85 stills. GREEN 36 · AMBER 49.** The lake with steep mountains, a kayak fleet, a log raft, river swimming, a rugby-style game in a dusty clearing, and the most striking *young men* frames in the archive: profile portraits with blue and red face paint and bandanas, the line of packs on a mossy path, huge backpacks on teenagers. Almost all of that is AMBER — this is the album to take to Dorian for approvals. GREEN here is water, the men at the grill, the game wide, and a handful of fine adult portraits.

**a4 · YMAW 15 (2015) — 88 stills. GREEN 55 · AMBER 24 · FLAG 1 · CEREMONY 8.** Mossy old-growth trails, a gravel clearing with a Canadian flag and a giant log ring, kayaks on a grey lake, crawling Quests up fern hills and through a trench, push-ups in a line, the dome frame going up. It also holds the archive's most explicit ceremony frames: handshake lines, arms-linked rows, a black-tarp lodge with young men entering — all CEREMONY. A young woman hiking (a4-007) is FLAG. Excellent adult portraits (the man on the mossy stump, the leaf sprig profile, the exhausted man on the log).

**a5 · YMAW 2014 Photos — 74 stills. GREEN 50 · AMBER 23 · CEREMONY 1.** Sunrise mist on the lake, a gravel clearing, a mobile kitchen truck, a shallow creek, men in folding chairs, the dusty scrum, and the best single "walk in" frame in the archive (a5-040, single file into sunlit forest, from behind). Strong on production men and on wide, sunlit camp life. One circle with a man reading from a red booklet is CEREMONY.

**a6 · Young Men's Adventure Weekend 2012 — 25 stills. GREEN 20 · AMBER 5.** Canvas teepees, the field-kitchen trailer and yellow box truck, polo-shirted men by the lake, yellow headbands. Small, mostly infrastructure and men — nearly all usable.

**a7 · YMAW 2013 — 32 stills. GREEN 13 · AMBER 14 · FLAG 4 · CEREMONY 1.** The bus, a cargo net, a canoe frame on the beach, four men with staffs on the shore. A girl appears in four frames (bus and camp) so parts of this album look like a mixed or family day; a man being pulled up from the ground is CEREMONY. Thin for the site beyond a7-006 and the men.

**a8 · YMAW Old Photos — 63 stills, undated. GREEN 30 · AMBER 31 · FLAG 2.** The early years at a big lake under a prominent mountain: a line of men and young men with wooden shields and spears, flour-cloud games, wheelbarrow races, river crossings on a rope, jumping off the shore, an inukshuk, two silhouettes at twilight. The young men in these are men now and can consent for themselves — so the AMBER here is the easiest to clear, and the "then and now" material lives here. Two frames with a girl are FLAG.
