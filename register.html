Participation agreement (v2026-1)

The Young Men's Adventure Weekend is a supervised outdoor program for young men aged 12 to 17, run by the Young Men's Adventure Weekend Society of BC through a trained, screened volunteer production team. The weekend includes travel by bus, a hike into a wilderness site, team camp building, quest stations that test mind and body, group circles, and camping outdoors in all weather.

I understand that outdoor activities carry inherent risks, including injury, and that YMAW manages these risks through trained leadership, safety briefings, appropriate equipment and supervision of every activity. I confirm the young man or men I am registering are able to take part in a physically active outdoor weekend, and that the medical information I have provided is complete and accurate.

I authorize the production team to obtain emergency medical care if needed, and to contact the person named as emergency contact.

I understand registration is refundable up to 14 days before the weekend begins, and transferable to another participant at any time by arrangement with the organizers.

YMAW Standards (2026)

Safety. We are all responsible for our own safety and the safety of others. See something unsafe, take charge and say so.

Buddy system. Never be alone. Every man is visible to at least one other man at all times, and never alone with a young man.

Clear. No drugs, alcohol or tobacco at YMAW or in the 24 hours before any YMAW event.

Honour the site. Leave it better than we found it.

Confidentiality. What you see and hear stays at the weekend. Speak to your own experience only.

Be on time. No foul language. We say "young men". We are modelling behaviour.

Don't make anything up. Get clear direction, set ego aside, tell your manager if you have misled anyone or any process.

For parents

Asked every year.

Is my son safe at YMAW?

Safety runs the weekend. Every activity is supervised by trained, screened volunteer staff, briefed before it starts, and matched to age and ability. Staff are never alone with a young man. The program has run on that discipline since 1990.

Does he need outdoor experience?

No. Quests and games are built so every young man can learn and win at his own pace, whatever his fitness or experience.

What does the $320 cover?

Everything: transport from the pickup points, all meals from Friday snacks to Sunday lunch, camping accommodation, all activities and equipment, and supervision from the bus stop on Friday to the bus stop on Sunday.

What if the cost is hard for our family right now?

Choose "Request financial assistance" when you register. We follow up personally and privately. No young man misses the weekend over money.

What should he bring?

A full packing list comes with your confirmation. In short: weather-appropriate clothing, a sleeping bag, personal items, and a willingness to try something new. Phones stay home.

What actually happens over the three days?

Friday the bus brings the young men to the site and they hike in, form teams and build their own camp. Saturday is the Quests: stations that test mind and body, then a circle in the evening. Sunday brings a closing ceremony, a rowdy staff-versus-young-men game, and the bus home.

Creating healthy communities by building strong, successful, spirited young men. Since 1990.
