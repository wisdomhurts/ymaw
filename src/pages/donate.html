Donate and Support

Send a young man who could not otherwise go.

Every dollar of the $320 goes into the weekend itself: the bus, the food, the site, the gear. Nobody is paid. When a family cannot cover it, the difference comes from people like you.

Give

By e-transfer, to one address.

Send an Interac e-transfer from your bank to the address below. Put "donation" in the message, and your name if you would like us to know who it came from. If you are sponsoring a particular young man, put his name instead and we will apply it to his registration.

E-transfer to info@ymaw.com Message: "donation" or the young man's name

Your support helps ensure that a young man who might not otherwise have the chance can step into this experience. A gift of $320 sends one young man for the whole weekend. Smaller gifts pool toward the same thing.

Questions about giving, or about a larger gift: info@ymaw.com.

Give time

Can't make the weekend? There is still a job with your name on it.

Truck load-up

The Thursday before the weekend, September 10. Everything the weekend needs goes into one truck, and it takes many hands.

Strike team offload

Sunday evening after the weekend, in Abbotsford. Meet the truck, unload it, and put the weekend back in the locker.

Shop for supplies

Help prep the gear and essentials for both the main weekend and the Friday staff training.

Spread the word

Tell a family with a young man aged 12 to 17. Share the site. Most young men arrive because a man they knew told their parents about it.

To take one of these on, tick it under "availability" when you register as production, or email info@ymaw.com.

Sponsor a young man you know

Bring him yourself.

The most direct way to support a young man is to be the man who brings him. A sponsor, whether father, uncle, guardian or family friend, registers alongside the young men he is responsible for, pays the same $320 each, and comes as part of the production team.

You will complete the screening and the Friday training like every other man, and you will spend the weekend in service to all of the young men, not only yours.
